# Design System: Clean Sky & White Light Mode

## Visual Hierarchy & Palette
- **Canvas / Root Background**: `#ffffff` (Pure White) with subtle sky-tinted page backdrop `#f0f7ff` / `#f8fafc`.
- **Card Surfaces**: Pure white `#ffffff` elevated with soft borderless tonal contrast against `#f0f7ff` or subtle shadow.
- **Primary Text**: Deep Charcoal / Slate `#0f172a` (WCAG AAA contrast > 12:1).
- **Secondary / Meta Text**: Slate Gray `#64748b` and Deep Sky `#0369a1`.
- **Primary Brand / Action Accent**: Sky Blue (`#0ea5e9`, hover `#0284c7`).
- **Threat Badges**:
  - Malicious / Critical: High-contrast Sky Blue `#0284c7` background with crisp white text `#ffffff`.
  - Benign: Soft sky tint `#e0f2fe` background with deep sky blue text `#0369a1`.
- **Borders**: Zero borders (`border: none !important`).

## Typography
- **Headings & Data**: Inter (`wght@600`, `wght@700`).
- **IPs, Protocols, Timestamps, Latency**: JetBrains Mono (`wght@400`, `wght@600`).
