# Product

## Register
Product

## Target Users & Purpose
- **Users**: Security operations analysts, academic researchers (University of Uyo), and network engineers evaluating edge-class network intrusion detection.
- **Purpose**: Real-time traffic flow classification, immediate threat triage and incident acknowledgement, and edge hardware telemetry monitoring under simulated resource constraints.

## Brand Personality & Aesthetic
- **Tone**: Analytical, calm, crisp, high-contrast, professional cybersecurity console.
- **Theme**: Clean light mode with pure white surfaces, soft sky blue accents, and deep slate typography for maximum readability.
- **Anti-References**: Muddy dark themes, unreadable low-contrast grays, claustrophobic card grids, neon clutter.

## Design Principles
1. **Clarity First**: Instant visibility of perimeter status, security incidents, and hardware constraints.
2. **Effortless Triage**: One-click acknowledgement with clear, unmistakable visual feedback.
3. **Restrained Accentuation**: Sky blue (`#0284c7`, `#0ea5e9`, `#38bdf8`) used intentionally for focal actions and telemetry.
